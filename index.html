Why this matters to academic operations

Room demand can change as course times change. This prototype highlights peak simultaneous demand, making it easier to explore scheduling capacity and identify potential shortfalls before publishing a schedule.

Scope: This demo measures simultaneous demand and compares it with an assumed room count. It does not assign specific rooms or model capacity, equipment, accessibility, instructor conflicts, or actual Workday Student functionality. All classes are fictional.

Created as an independent exploration of scheduling validation, inspired by a Python minimum-meeting-rooms algorithm. Intended as a discussion starter for the Workday Project Support Assistant application.
